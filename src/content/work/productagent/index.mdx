## Overview

An AI-powered product research tool exploring Retrieval-Augmented Generation and AI agents. You input an image of a product; the pipeline collects reviews and feedback for that product and its competitors and turns them into actionable insights.

Agents validate and extract relevant information from official product manuals, which are embedded into a vector database. Manuals plus user feedback feed a RAG system that generates targeted design suggestions — e.g. "Increase maximum table height from 70 cm to 76 cm."

## Reflection

The assignment was meant to introduce APIs and LLMs through a basic review system; I took it further into a full agentic RAG pipeline. The agent ecosystem was fragmented at the time (LangGraph, LangChain, OpenAI agents, pre-MCP). Midway, Google ADK launched — I got a test case running, but integration caused instability, so I reverted to LangChain agents. A lesson in adapting to a fast-moving landscape.
