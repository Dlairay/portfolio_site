## Overview

EncoreWorks matches experienced professionals with companies that need proven expertise, through AI-powered, project-based engagements.

Professionals tell the platform about themselves through an AI-guided onboarding conversation, get matched on skills, motivations, work style and availability, then connect directly with companies. A human team checks the quality of every match.
