## Overview

Every product launch is a guess. SIMETRY builds a synthetic population of Singapore consumers from real population data, puts them in a room with your product, and lets them talk each other into — or out of — buying it.

The output is what a brand needs before launch: likely adoption, willingness to pay, the objections that come up, and the gap between what people say in public and what they privately think.

## How it works

- Agents are seeded from the Nemotron-Personas-Singapore dataset, each with its own traits such as price sensitivity and trust in peers, influencers and ads
- Every tick, each agent perceives, plans, writes to memory and occasionally reflects — a generative-agents loop run through LiteLLM so it can use OpenAI, Ollama or on-device MLX models
- A world model injects market events such as price cuts, influencer mentions and supply shocks; social spread is modelled as a graph
- Postgres with pgvector acts as a semantic cache for decisions, cutting repeat LLM calls
- Every decision is logged, and a React replay viewer shows the room, speech bubbles, private thoughts and a final report

## The site

simetry.ai is a WebGL scroll film built with React Three Fiber: a product is picked, a particle human forms around it, a focus group argues over it, and the camera pulls back to a grid of 1,000 simulated rooms. Figures are particle clouds sampled from posed meshes and baked ahead of time. Weaker GPUs are detected and get a lightweight non-WebGL version.
