## Overview

We redesigned a vegetation trimming robot whose original form was bulky and intimidating, aiming for something that feels at home in a park. Early concepts had two arms; we realised one was enough — which sparked the idea of an arm that grows out like a sprout, leading to a seed-inspired body that harmonises with nature.

## Reflection

Learned Fusion 360 in depth, and used AI in ideation and iteration.
