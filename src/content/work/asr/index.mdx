## Overview

For a hackathon, I fine-tuned OpenAI's whisper-medium.en and accelerated it with FasterWhisper (up to 4× faster inference). The model balanced size, speed and accuracy — crucial when integrated with the other modules. The team didn't win, but my model ranked 5th of 60 groups.

## Reflection

My first time with ASR and fine-tuning — a steep curve for two weeks. I first tried NVIDIA Parakeet V2, but meaningful fine-tuning on NeMo required NVIDIA microservices beyond my budget, so I switched to Hugging Face.
